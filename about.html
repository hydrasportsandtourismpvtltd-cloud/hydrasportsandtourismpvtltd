Built on discipline.
Driven by the sea and the ghats.

Hydra Sports & Tourism Pvt. Ltd. began as a single roller-skating rink in Kerala and grew into a record-setting sports academy and adventure tourism company — without ever losing sight of the kid learning to fall, get up, and try again.

Our Story

From one rink to 16 sports

Hydra started with a simple idea: Kerala's kids deserved coaching that took them as seriously as any national academy would. What began as roller-skating lessons for a handful of neighbourhood children has grown into a company running programs across 16 sports and 18+ partner schools.

Along the way, we picked up something we didn't set out for — five Guinness World Records and 22 individual record holders. But the achievement we're proudest of is quieter: thousands of first-time athletes who found out they were capable of more than they thought.

In parallel, our guides started taking students' families out on the backwaters and hills around us — and that side of the work grew into Hydra Tourism, now offering kayaking, boating, ATV rides and mud tourism across Kerala.

"We don't coach sport in isolation from life. Discipline on the court is discipline everywhere else, too."

— Hydra Founding Team

What Drives Us

Mission, Vision & Values

Three commitments that shape every class we teach and every trip we run.

Mission

To inspire and train young talents to achieve excellence through discipline, dedication, and teamwork — regardless of where they start from.

Vision

To become a leading sports and tourism company in Kerala — nurturing future champions with strong values and a lifelong love of movement.

Values

Patience over pressure, individual attention over one-size-fits-all, and honesty about progress — even when it's slow.

Our Journey

Milestones along the way

A quick look at how a single rink grew into a sports and tourism company.

Y1

Roller skating rink opens

Hydra's first coaching program starts with a handful of students and one discipline: roller skating.

Y2

Expansion into team sports

Football, basketball and cricket coaching added as demand grows beyond the rink.

Y3

First Guinness World Record

A Hydra athlete sets the company's first world record, putting Kerala's academy on the national map.

Y4

18+ school partnerships

Hydra coaching programs are adopted across partner schools throughout Kerala.

Y5

Hydra Tourism launches

Kayaking and boating trips begin, followed by mud tourism and ATV adventures across Kerala's backwaters and hills.
