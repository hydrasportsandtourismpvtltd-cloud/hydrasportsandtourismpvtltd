About The Program

Karate, Done Right

Karate is one of Hydra's flagship disciplines — trained under the same system that has produced 5 Guinness World Records. Our academy trains practitioners of every age, from first-timers learning basic stances to competitive athletes chasing state and national titles. Sessions cover kata forms, kumite sparring, discipline, and belt-grading preparation, all under close, patient supervision.

Training Path

Beginner to Advanced

Every student follows a structured path — no one is rushed, no one is held back.

LEVEL 01

Beginner

  • Basic stances, blocks and punches
  • Introductory kata and etiquette
  • Falling technique and basic kumite rules
  • Building confidence and discipline, step by step
LEVEL 02

Intermediate

  • Intermediate kata and combination techniques
  • Controlled kumite sparring drills
  • Belt-grading preparation and form refinement
  • Strength and flexibility building
LEVEL 03

Advanced

  • Competitive kumite strategy and advanced kata
  • Precision striking and counter techniques
  • District, state and national tournament preparation
  • Personalised coaching toward record attempts
