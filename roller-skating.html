About The Program

Roller Skating, Done Right

Roller skating is Hydra's flagship discipline — the sport that put us on the map with 5 Guinness World Records. Our academy trains skaters of every age, from first-timers finding their balance to competitive athletes chasing state and national titles. Training Path

Beginner to Advanced

Every skater follows a structured path — no one is rushed, no one is held back.

LEVEL 01

Beginner

  • Gear fitting, safety basics and falling technique
  • Balance, posture and first independent glides
  • Stopping techniques and turning basics
  • Building confidence on wheels, step by step
LEVEL 02

Intermediate

  • Speed skating fundamentals and stride efficiency
  • Crossovers, cornering and controlled acceleration
  • Introductory artistic skating movements
  • Endurance building and race-pace drills
LEVEL 03

Advanced

  • Competitive race strategy and pack skating
  • Advanced artistic routines and choreography
  • District, state and national tournament preparation
  • Personalised coaching toward record attempts
